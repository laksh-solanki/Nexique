Frequently Asked Questions

How does the NFC smart integration work?

Each card from our premium NFC or Metal series features an embedded NTAG213 microchip. When you tap the card on any modern iPhone or Android device, it triggers an instant NFC pop-up directing the recipient to your customizable landing page, digital portfolio, vCard file, or social links. No apps, installations, or logins are required by either you or the recipient.

Can I design custom shape outlines or die-cuts?

Yes, custom die-cut outlines are fully supported in our Bespoke Metal Legacy package. This allows us to introduce customized hollow segments, geometric cutouts, or brand logo silhouettes directly through the steel or aluminum sheet. For paper-based cards, custom die-cut shapes can be requested during your design consultation.

What is the average turnaround and shipping time?

For traditional hot-foil paper cards, design proofing takes 2-3 business days, and physical crafting requires 5-7 business days. Our Bespoke Metal Legacy series takes 10-14 business days due to the advanced laser engraving and edge treatment needed. Standard shipping is free globally, while expedited overnight shipping can be arranged.

What if I do not have a finalized design ready?

Do not worry! Every order includes designer collaboration. If you have a logo or sketch, our boutique design lab will structure your business, greeting, or invitation card typography and layout. Under the Bespoke Metal Legacy tier, we conduct a full 1-on-1 consultation to develop your layout from scratch.

Are the landing pages customizable after printing?

Absolutely. The web link on the NFC chip is mapped to our redirect server. You can modify your destination URL, update phone details, edit website links, and change your bio information anytime directly through your CardFesta Account dashboard—without needing to print new physical cards.
